01 // ABOUT
A bit about me

I graduated from RMIT University with a Bachelor of IT and a Diploma of IT. Most of what I know came from building things — not just reading about them.

My home lab runs Windows Server 2022 on Hyper-V with a full Active Directory setup — OUs, user accounts, security groups, password resets, account management. I built it because I wanted the experience, not because I had to.

During my degree I worked with real AWS infrastructure (IAM, KMS, S3, EC2), configured firewalls on Kali Linux, and studied cryptography, threat analysis, and risk management — all while working part-time in busy, people-facing environments.

Long-term I want to move into cybersecurity — specifically SOC analysis and threat detection. But I believe in building a proper foundation first. IT support is where that starts.
